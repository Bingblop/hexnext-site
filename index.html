HexNext-QS-Notifications-Module

System UI paints every Quick panel tile one colour and keeps pictures off notification cards, so overlays alone cannot show them the way the theme previews do. This optional LSPosed module keeps a theme's full-colour tile glyphs, puts its art behind your notifications, colours the status bar icons and shows a pack's art on the brightness and volume sliders.
