#Hex Next

A theming app for Samsung phones running One UI 7, 8 and 9. Colours, icon packs, fonts and backgrounds for the system apps, built on your device.

  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of service
  • Source and releases

#Hex Next needs root (UID 0) or, if root is not an option, system access (UID 1000) on a Samsung One UI device; both give the full feature set. Shizuku over wireless debugging applies the accent colour only. It is not affiliated with Samsung or Google.
