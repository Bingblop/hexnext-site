One UI 7 and 8 test

Thank you for helping. This test shows how One UI 7 and One UI 8 name their colours and pictures, so #Hex Next can theme those versions properly. It takes about 20 minutes, and most of that is waiting. You do not need to know anything about rooting or terminals: do the steps in order, exactly as written.

Only do this on your own phone, and only if it is rooted right now. The test theme is applied in Test mode, so a restart removes it completely. If the screen ever freezes or looks wrong, hold the Side button and the Volume down button together for about 10 seconds, until the phone restarts. After the restart the phone looks normal again. With temporary root, a restart also ends root until you root the phone again.

What you need

  • A Samsung Galaxy phone on One UI 7 or One UI 8. To check, open Settings › About phone › Software information and read the line One UI version.
  • Root that works right now, with KernelSU, Magisk or APatch. If the phone was restarted after you last rooted it, root it again first.
  • Wi-Fi, at least 30% battery and about 1 GB of free storage.
  • About 20 minutes.

What the test reads, and what it never reads

It reads: technical files from Samsung's own system apps (the names of their colours and icons), Samsung's colour palette files, #Hex Next's own logs, the phone's model and software version, and about 16 pictures of system screens that it takes by itself: the Quick panel, Display settings, Sound settings, the App info page of #Hex Next, the phone keypad, the volume slider and the terminal with the keyboard.

It never opens your photos, messages, contacts, accounts or passwords, and it does not list your apps. Where a file would name another app, such as an overlay for it, a #Hex Next App icons overlay or a log line about one, the test leaves that out or writes "(app)" instead, and only counts them. An e-mail address or a chat id in a crash line is written "(id)". Each picture comes with the screen's layout: where each part sits, without the words on the screen. Of your root modules, only #Hex Next's, LSPosed and Zygisk are named; the others are counted. The test sends nothing anywhere. It makes one file, and you decide to send it.

The Quick panel picture shows what is on your Quick panel at that moment, such as the name of your Wi-Fi network, a connected Bluetooth device or the song that is playing. Part 1 clears your notifications, so none of them are in the picture.

Part 1: Get the phone ready

  1. Turn on Samsung's colour palette. Open Settings. Tap Wallpaper and style. Tap Color palette. If there is a switch at the top, turn it on. Tap any one of the colour sets. Tap Apply. If you already use a colour palette, leave it as it is.
  2. Clear your notifications. Swipe down from the top of the screen and tap Clear.
  3. Stay on Wi-Fi until the test is finished.

Part 2: Install #Hex Next Beta and apply the test theme

Open this page on the phone you are testing, then tap the button.

Download #Hex Next Beta

  1. If the browser asks, tap Download (or Download anyway).
  2. When the download is finished, tap Open. No Open button? Open the My Files app, tap Downloads and tap the file whose name starts with HexNext-Beta.
  3. If the phone says it is not allowed to install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source and tap the back arrow.
  4. Tap Install. If a Google Play Protect box appears, tap More details and then Install anyway.
  5. Tap Open. The app is called #Hex Next Beta.
  6. The app opens a guide called Set up Hex Next. Go through its pages like this:
    • Welcome, AI themes and Your phone: tap Next on each.
    • Root or Shizuku: tap Request root access.
      • Magisk: a box appears. Tap Grant.
      • KernelSU or APatch: no box appears. Open your root app, tap Superuser, find #Hex Next Beta in the list and turn its switch on. Go back to #Hex Next Beta and tap Request root access again.
      When the page says Connected, tap Next.
    • Notifications: tap Allow notifications, then Allow. Tap Next.
    • Test mode first: tap Next.
    • Scan your phone: tap Scan. Wait about one minute, until it says Phone scanned. Tap Next.
    • Starting pack: do not choose a pack. Leave it on Colours only and tap Next.
    • System UI hook: tap Next.
    • All set: tap Done.
  7. You now see the main screen. Near the bottom there is a ticked box that says Test Mode: Overlays reset on reboot. It is locked on. That is correct.
  8. Tap Apply theme at the bottom right. Wait. It takes one to three minutes and shows a progress bar, and the screen may flicker once or twice. It is finished when you see Theme applied. Do not tap anything else in the app.

Some colours on the phone now look different. That is the test theme, and Part 5 removes it. If the app says Couldn't apply the theme instead, that is useful to know as well: carry on with Part 3, and the test records what went wrong.

Part 3: Run the test

  1. Install Termux, a free terminal app. Open the Play Store, search for Termux, tap Install and then Open. Not in your Play Store? Get it from F-Droid's Termux page: tap Download APK there and install it the same way you installed #Hex Next Beta.
  2. In Termux, wait until you see a line that ends with $ and a blinking block. The first start can take a minute.
  3. Give Termux root.
    • KernelSU or APatch: open your root app, tap Superuser, find Termux in the list and turn its switch on. Then go back to Termux.
    • Magisk: nothing to do yet. A box asks you in step 5.
  4. Copy this command. Tap Copy:
    curl -fsSL https://bingblop.github.io/hexnext-site/oneui-test.sh -o t.sh && su -c "sh $PWD/t.sh"
  5. Go back to Termux. Touch and hold the black screen until a small menu appears, then tap Paste. The command appears. Tap the Enter key on the keyboard (the key at the bottom right). Magisk: when the box appears, tap Grant.
  6. Put the phone down and do not touch it. For 5 to 10 minutes the screen changes by itself: it opens the Quick panel, Settings and the phone keypad, and it switches between light and dark mode once. That is the test taking its pictures. The screen stays on by itself.
  7. The test is finished when Termux is back on the screen and says DONE.

Part 4: Send the result

  1. The result is one file in your Downloads folder. Open the My Files app and tap Downloads. The file's name starts with HexNextTest and ends with .tar.gz. Termux shows the full name under the word DONE.
  2. Send that file to the person who gave you this page. Is it too big to attach? Upload it to Google Drive, set sharing to Anyone with the link and send the link.

Part 5: Put your phone back

  1. Open #Hex Next Beta. Tap Remove at the bottom left, then tap Remove again in the box that asks. Wait until it says Theme removed.
  2. If you turned the colour palette on only for this test, turn it off again: Settings › Wallpaper and style › Color palette.
  3. You can now uninstall #Hex Next Beta and Termux if you do not want to keep them: touch and hold the app's icon and tap Uninstall. After you have sent the result file, you can delete it from Downloads.

If something goes wrong

  • Termux says "This test needs root, and it did not get root." Do step 3 of Part 3, then paste the command again.
  • Termux says "curl: not found" or "command not found". Type pkg install -y curl, tap Enter and wait until the $ line is back. Then paste the command again.
  • Termux says "ALMOST DONE. One thing is missing:". Do what the line under it says, then paste the command again. If you cannot do it, send the file anyway.
  • The screen went dark or the phone locked during the test. Unlock it and open Termux again. The test carries on by itself. Wait for DONE.
  • The phone will not install the app and names Auto Blocker. Open Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker and turn it off. Install the app, then turn Auto Blocker on again after Part 5.
  • #Hex Next Beta says the beta has ended. Tell the person who gave you this page. They will send you a newer build.
  • You restarted the phone in the middle. With temporary root, root the phone again. Then open #Hex Next Beta, tap Apply theme, wait for Theme applied and do Part 3 from step 5.

Still stuck? Take a screenshot of what you see and send it to the person who gave you this page.
